# Cath lab display imagery

`coronary-simulation-atlas.png` was generated for this project's approved cath lab design concept using OpenAI image generation. It contains three synthetic fluoroscopy-style illustrations, not real patient scans or validated diagnostic projections.

The texture is used only as scenery on the 3D room's monitors and is labelled as illustrative simulation. It must not be used to assess clinical anatomy, diagnose disease, or replace the clinical cases' teaching images. The room retains procedural illustrations if the atlas cannot load.
