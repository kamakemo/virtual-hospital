# Patient head scan

`head.glb`, `head-color.jpg`, `head-normal.jpg`

3D head scan of Lee Perry-Smith by Infinite-Realities (https://ir-ltd.net/),
licensed under the Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 Unported License
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/). Files as distributed with
three.js (examples/models/gltf/LeePerrySmith, release r170).

Changes made in the app: the bust is trimmed at the neck at load time, tinted
for different skin tones, and a hair shell and theatre cap are generated from
its scalp surface.
