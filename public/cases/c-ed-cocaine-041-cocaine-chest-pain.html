🧭 The toxidrome that reframes everything

The constellation — tachycardia, hypertension, mydriasis, hyperthermia, diaphoresis, agitation — is a textbook sympathomimetic toxidrome. Recognising it tells you the pain is being driven by a catecholamine surge and coronary vasospasm, and it flags the drugs that will help (benzodiazepines, nitrates, cooling) and the drug that will harm (a beta-blocker).

⚠️ The trap of "just control the numbers"A tachycardic, hypertensive young man looks like he needs rate and pressure control. But the driver is central sympathetic outflow — sedate that with a benzodiazepine and the numbers fall on their own. Reach for a beta-blocker and you strip away beta-2 vasodilation, leaving alpha-vasoconstriction unopposed — the pressure climbs and the coronaries clamp.

👨‍⚕️ Resident Review

The EM resident thinks out loud at 23:52:

🧠 Frame it as toxicology AND cardiology at once

"This is cocaine-associated chest pain until proven otherwise. My first drug isn't a cardiac drug in the classic sense — it's a benzodiazepine to switch off the sympathetic storm. That single move lowers his heart rate, his blood pressure and his coronary spasm together. Then GTN for the spasm, aspirin is already on board, and I'll actively cool him. I will not give a beta-blocker. I'll get serial ECGs and troponin, and keep the aorta in mind."

🎯 Her order of operations

  1. 💊 IV benzodiazepine (e.g. diazepam/lorazepam), titrated to calm — treat agitation and you treat the coronaries.
  2. 💉 GTN for ongoing ischaemic pain / hypertension (relieves vasospasm), with aspirin continued.
  3. 🌡️ Active cooling for hyperthermia; IV fluids; check for rhabdomyolysis (CK).
  4. 📈 Serial 12-leads — look for true STEMI, and for a wide QRS (sodium-channel toxicity → bicarbonate).
  5. 🚫 No beta-blocker acutely; refractory hypertension/spasm → phentolamine (alpha-blocker) or more nitrate/CCB.
⚔️ ONE-LINER RULE"In cocaine chest pain, benzodiazepines ARE the cardiac drug — calm the brain and you calm the coronaries. The sedative is doing your rate and pressure control for you."
🧪 Resident's self-testThe BP is 198/112 and the HR is 128 — is IV metoprolol a reasonable way to bring both down quickly?

No — this is the cardinal error. Blocking beta receptors removes beta-2-mediated vasodilation, so cocaine's alpha-mediated vasoconstriction acts unopposed: blood pressure paradoxically rises and coronary spasm worsens. Give a benzodiazepine (± GTN, ± phentolamine) instead. This is the same physiology that makes beta-before-alpha blockade dangerous in phaeochromocytoma.

🧠 Teaching Points + Interactive Simulation

🔬 The mechanism — WHY the pain, WHY the danger (the WHY behind the WHY)

  1. 🧠 Reuptake blockade: cocaine blocks presynaptic reuptake of noradrenaline and dopamine (and serotonin) → catecholamines flood the synapse → a whole-body sympathetic surge.
  2. 🫀 Coronary vasospasm: alpha-1 stimulation constricts the coronary arteries — the central, spasm-driven cause of the chest pain, on top of raised heart rate and blood pressure.
  3. 🩸 Thrombosis + demand: cocaine activates platelets and promotes thrombus, while tachycardia + hypertension + hyperthermia ramp up myocardial oxygen demand — supply falls as demand rises.
  4. ⚡ Sodium-channel block: cocaine is a local anaesthetic — it blocks cardiac fast sodium channels → QRS widening and ventricular arrhythmia (treated with bicarbonate), and it raises aortic-dissection risk.
⚔️ ONE-LINER RULE"Cocaine hits the heart four ways at once: it spasms the coronaries, clots them, drives up demand, and poisons the sodium channels. Your treatment has to answer all four — and never make the spasm worse."

⚖️ Contrast pair — Cocaine ACS vs classic atherothrombotic ACS

❄️ Cocaine ACS

  • Spasm + demand + platelet activation from a catecholamine surge
  • Benzodiazepines first, plus GTN, aspirin, cooling
  • Avoid beta-blockers (unopposed alpha)

🩸 Classic atherothrombotic ACS

  • Plaque rupture + occlusive thrombus
  • Antiplatelets, anticoagulation, PCI
  • Beta-blocker within 24 h once stable is standard

💡 Same symptom, different physiology — and the beta-blocker that helps one can harm the other. Ask "is this a stimulated heart or a blocked pipe?" before you prescribe.

📋 Orders

🖊️ The order set (23:50)

  • 💊 IV benzodiazepine (e.g. diazepam 5–10 mg or lorazepam), titrated to calm — the first and most important order.
  • 💉 GTN sublingual/infusion for ongoing pain and hypertension (avoid if hypotensive or recent PDE5 inhibitor).

👩‍⚕️ Nursing Care

🛏️ Resus-bay priorities — the nurse runs the safety net

  • 📟 Continuous telemetry, defib pads attached; watch for wide-complex tachycardia and VT/VF.
  • 💊 Titrate the benzodiazepine to a calm, rousable patient — re-dose as agitation returns; a settled patient has a settling heart.
  • 🌡️ Active cooling and frequent temperature checks — hyperthermia is a silent killer here (target < 38.5°C).
  • 📈 Repeat the ECG the moment pain or rhythm changes; flag any QRS widening to the team (bicarbonate territory).
  • 🩸 Cannulate ×2, send the troponin/CK/tox set, monitor BP every 5–15 min (watch for over-nitration).
  • 🚫 Challenge any beta-blocker order and any premature heparin before the aorta is considered — the nurse is the last line against the unopposed-alpha error.
⚔️ ONE-LINER RULE"The nurse who keeps the room quiet, the benzodiazepine titrated and the beta-blocker out of the drug tray is doing frontline cardiology."

🫀 Serial 12-leads — read the rate, the ST, and the QRS width

Fast sinus tachycardia early; the later complexes show a widening QRS — cocaine's sodium-channel blockade. A QRS > 100–120 ms after cocaine points you to sodium bicarbonate.

🩻 Adjunct imaging

  • Bedside echo (POCUS): regional wall-motion abnormality supports ischaemia; screens effusion/dissection flap.

S12

📟 Monitoring

📡 What to watch and why

  • 📈 Continuous telemetry with defib to hand — watch for wide-complex tachycardia / VT and QRS widening (bicarbonate trigger).
  • 🩸 BP and HR every 5–15 min — they should fall as the benzodiazepine takes effect; a rising BP after any beta-blocker is the unopposed-alpha signature.
  • 🌡️ Temperature — hyperthermia trends; keep cooling until < 38.5°C.
  • 🧪 Serial troponin (0/3 h) and repeat 12-leads with any symptom change; recheck CK/renal function for rhabdomyolysis.
  • 🧠 Sedation level / agitation — re-dose benzodiazepine before the storm rebuilds; balance against over-sedation.
⚔️ ONE-LINER RULE"Your best monitor of success is a calmer patient with a slowing heart and softening pressure — if those numbers climb after a drug, ask what you just gave."
S13

⚠️ Complications

💀 What can go wrong in the ED phase

  • 🫀 Myocardial infarction from spasm ± thrombus — real in a minority, and it can strike a young patient with clean-looking arteries.
  • ⚡ Ventricular arrhythmia / VT / VF — from sodium-channel blockade (wide QRS) and an irritable, ischaemic myocardium.
  • 🩸 Aortic dissection — cocaine + severe hypertension is a classic trigger; keep it in mind before anticoagulation.
  • 🌡️ Hyperthermia → rhabdomyolysis, seizures, DIC, multi-organ failure — the toxidrome kills beyond the heart.
  • 🚫 The iatrogenic catastrophe: a beta-blocker given "for the numbers" → unopposed alpha → hypertensive crisis + worsened coronary spasm/MI.
  • 🧠 Intracranial haemorrhage / stroke from the pressure surge; agitation-related injury.
⚔️ ONE-LINER RULE"The deadliest complication of cocaine chest pain is often the one you prescribe — treat the storm with sedation, not with a beta-blocker."

🔬 Advanced Topics — Fellowship Level

🧠 Beyond the pathway

  • 🧬 The four-hit mechanism in depth: cocaine blocks presynaptic dopamine and noradrenaline reuptake → synaptic catecholamine excess → alpha-1 coronary vasoconstriction, plus platelet activation and thrombosis, increased myocardial oxygen demand (rate/pressure/contractility), and — with chronic use — accelerated atherosclerosis and endothelial dysfunction. Four converging insults on coronary supply and demand.
  • 🩸 Cocaine and aortic dissection: the acute pressure/shear surge plus chronic vascular injury makes cocaine a recognised dissection trigger — a critical reason not to anticoagulate blindly, and to image the aorta when the story fits.
  • ⚡ Sodium-channel-blocking arrhythmia: cocaine's local-anaesthetic (class-I-like) action widens the QRS and can precipitate ventricular arrhythmia; sodium bicarbonate (a sodium load + alkalinisation) is the antidote, as in TCA toxicity. Terminal R wave in aVR is the ECG fingerprint.
  • 🦠 Levamisole-adulterant vasculitis: levamisole-contaminated cocaine causes agranulocytosis and a retiform/purpuric (ANCA-associated) vasculitis, classically of the ears — a systemic clue to the substance.
  • 📦 Body-packers & body-stuffers: concealed drug packets can rupture → catastrophic overdose. Surgical/decontamination decisions and imaging matter; avoid endoscopic retrieval that could rupture a packet.
  • 🌡️ Hyperthermia management: aggressive external/evaporative cooling, generous benzodiazepines, IV fluids, and monitoring for rhabdomyolysis, DIC and multi-organ failure — hyperthermia, not the chest pain, is often what actually kills.

🎭 Morbidity & Mortality Conference

🎭 M&M — "The Beta-Blocker That Clamped the Coronaries"

The case: A 26-year-old man arrived agitated and diaphoretic with crushing chest pain after a night of cocaine. HR 130, BP 200/120, pupils dilated. A busy team, wanting to "control the tachycardia and hypertension," gave IV metoprolol. Over the next fifteen minutes his blood pressure climbed to 220/130 and his anterior ST segments deepened as unopposed alpha vasoconstriction clamped his coronaries. He developed a hypertensive crisis and an evolving MI, and arrested in VF before phentolamine could be drawn up. No benzodiazepine had been given.

🧀 The Swiss-cheese holes that lined up:

🧠 REFLEX"High BP and pulse → beta-blocker" applied without asking WHY the numbers were high.
💥 TOXIDROME MISSEDAgitation, mydriasis, sweat, fever = sympathomimetic — the cue to sedate, not beta-block.
💊 NO BENZODIAZEPINEThe first-line drug that would have dropped HR, BP and spasm was never given.
🩸 UNOPPOSED ALPHABeta-2 vasodilation removed → alpha vasoconstriction ran unchecked → coronary tourniquet.
⏱️ NO RESCUE READYPhentolamine/nitrate not immediately available when the pressure paradoxically rose.

🛡️ What would have saved him: recognise the sympathomimetic toxidrome; give a benzodiazepine first to lower the whole catecholamine drive; add GTN to relieve spasm and phentolamine for refractory hypertension; treat the wide QRS with bicarbonate; and never give an acute beta-blocker — it is the same beta-before-alpha physiology that kills in phaeochromocytoma. This is exactly the discipline that saved the patient in Bed 11.

⚔️ ONE-LINER RULE"'Give a beta-blocker for the numbers' has killed cocaine patients who only needed a benzodiazepine and a nitrate. Ask why the numbers are high before you lower them."
